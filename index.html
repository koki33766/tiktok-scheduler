FAQ

Questions, answered

Is PostFlow safe? Does it need my TikTok password?
Never. PostFlow uses TikTok Login Kit — the official OAuth flow. You log in on TikTok's own page, and TikTok issues PostFlow a limited-scope token. We never see or store your password, and you can revoke access anytime in your TikTok settings under Security → Manage app permissions.
How does the posting actually work?
PostFlow uses TikTok's official Content Posting API. When your scheduled time arrives, your video is submitted directly to TikTok's servers with your caption, hashtags, and privacy setting. No browser automation or unofficial tricks are involved.
What data does PostFlow collect?
Only the minimum needed to operate: your TikTok user ID and OAuth token (session only, cleared on logout), plus the video and caption you schedule — which are transmitted to TikTok and not retained. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.
Can I schedule to multiple accounts?
Each PostFlow login connects one TikTok account — your own. Posting is always to the account you authorized, in line with TikTok's Developer Platform Policy.
What video formats are supported?
MP4 and MOV files up to 500MB, in any aspect ratio TikTok accepts (9:16 recommended). Videos are streamed directly to TikTok at publish time and never stored on our servers.
What happens if TikTok's API is down at my scheduled time?
PostFlow automatically retries for up to 30 minutes and notifies you of the final status. Your video is never silently dropped.
